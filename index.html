Dynamic trend prediction of tidal-gate discharge潮汐港闸流量动态趋势预测

First-author paper & associated patent research第一作者论文与伴生专利研究

Extracting dynamic information from limited hydrological observations.从有限水文观测中提取动态变化信息。

Combines two water-level inputs and four temporal-change features in a BP neural network to predict a dynamic discharge coefficient, then calculates flow using the weir equation. Evaluated at four tidal gates with dedicated analysis of operational transitions.将两项水位与四项变化率作为 BP 神经网络输入，预测动态出流系数，再通过堰流公式计算流量。在四座潮汐港闸开展评价，并单独分析开关闸瞬间的表现。

9.73% → 1.32%mean relative error in 2006, paper Table 72006 年平均相对误差，论文表 7
Method & results方法与结果

The research uses a 6–5–1 BP network and compares the dynamic trend method with the traditional weir-flow calculation. The portfolio presents input construction, reported training settings, five-fold validation, individual opening/closing events and four operational scenarios.研究采用 6–5–1 BP 网络，与传统堰流计算比较。项目展示输入构造、训练参数、五折验证、启闭时刻样本及四类运行工况的结果。

02Research project科研项目2022–2025

Taihu Basin river networks太湖流域河网与洪涝风险

Core modelling & analysis contributor核心建模与分析成员

Connecting river-network change to flood response.从河网变化理解洪水响应。

Updated network topology and gate–pump operating conditions in a TBM model containing 1,458 channels, 10,682 cross-sections and 477 hydraulic structures. Compared three operating strategies across 44 key sections.在包含 1,458 条河道、10,682 个计算断面及 477 座水工建筑物的 TBM 模型中，更新河网拓扑与闸泵运行条件，对比 3 种调度方案下 44 个关键断面的水位响应。

1,458modelled channels条建模河道
Methods & outcome方法与结果

Peak water-level absolute errors at three stations were 2–6 cm during the 2018 validation period. Prepared the Huxi-area technical report and integrated results from three study areas into the final project report.2018 年验证期 3 个站点的洪峰水位绝对误差为 2–6 cm。独立编制湖西区技术报告，并整合 3 个典型区成果形成结题报告。

TBM · River networks · Scenario analysis

Project lead项目负责人

Testing river and drainage-system response.评估河流与排水系统的工况响应。

Built a 1D HEC-RAS model of a 9 km reach of the Rönne River and compared four discharge–sea-level combinations. Used MIKE+ to examine drainage response under 85% impervious cover, 1.3× rainfall and varying outlet levels.使用 HEC-RAS 建立 Rönne River 下游 9 km 河段一维模型，对比 4 组流量—海水位情景；使用 MIKE+ 分析 85% 不透水面积、1.3 倍降雨和不同出口水位下的排水响应。

9km modelled river reachkm 建模河段

Examined nonlinear water-level responses and downstream backwater effects. Compared pipe resizing and storage-tank location and size to assess potential reductions in surcharge and overflow risk.识别非线性水位响应与下游回水影响，比较管径调整、调蓄池位置与尺寸变化对管网高水位和检查井溢流风险的影响。

Extreme floods & lake storage极端洪水与湖泊调蓄

Extreme-event analysis lead极端事件分析负责人

From antecedent conditions to flood attenuation.从前期蓄水条件到洪峰削减。

Evaluated 88 rainfall and snowmelt scenarios for a 340 km² catchment using a conceptual rainfall–runoff model and shared group-calibrated parameters. Examined antecedent storage, snow availability and event timing.基于概念性降雨—径流模型与小组共同率定参数，对 340 km² 集水区评估 88 组暴雨与融雪情景，结合前期蓄水、积雪可用量与事件日期解释响应差异。

88rainfall & snowmelt scenarios组暴雨与融雪情景
Methods & outcome方法与结果

Used Python for lake flood routing with surface areas of 8, 16 and 32 km². For the selected events, the 16 km² scenario produced approximately 49–57% simulated peak reduction; this result is specific to those scenarios.使用 Python 对 8、16、32 km² 湖面面积进行洪水演算。在选定事件中，16 km² 情景的模拟削峰率约为 49%–57%；该结果仅对应这些测试情景。

06Course project课程项目Autumn 2026 / 2026 秋季

Outfall engineering design排放工程设计与比选

Project lead项目负责人

Balancing dilution, hydraulics and cost.平衡稀释要求、水力条件与成本。

Compared 15 nozzle–tunnel combinations for a 20 m³/s cooling-water discharge, considering dilution, head loss and annualised cost. Evaluated stratified mixing for a separate 6 m³/s marine wastewater outfall.围绕 20 m³/s 冷却水排放，在稀释要求、水头损失与年化成本约束下比较 15 组喷嘴—隧洞组合；另对 6 m³/s 海洋污水排放分析分层混合过程。

15design combinations compared组设计组合比选
Methods & outcome方法与结果

Under the course design and cost assumptions, 10 nozzles and a 20 m² main tunnel provided the lowest-cost tested option. The marine-outfall calculations considered 306 nozzles, a 915 m diffuser and two density profiles.在课程给定设计与成本假设下，10 个喷嘴、20 m² 主隧洞为测试方案中成本最低的组合。海洋排放计算涉及 306 个喷嘴、915 m 扩散器及两组密度剖面。

Outfall design · Dilution · Cost comparison
